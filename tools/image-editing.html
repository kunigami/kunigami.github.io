Image workspace

Drop an image to add and select it. In Edit mode, drag images to move them and drag corners to resize. Hold Shift while resizing to change the aspect ratio. Arrow keys move the selection one pixel; Shift and arrow keys move it ten pixels. Click empty space or press Escape to deselect. In Drag mode, drag anywhere to pan. Scrolling pans in either mode.
