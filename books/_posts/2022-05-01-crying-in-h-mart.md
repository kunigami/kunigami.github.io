<b>Crying in H Mart</b> (Audiobook) by Michelle Zauner. It's a memoir by Zauner, a half-white half-Korean singer of the band <i>Japanese Breakfast</i>. It recounts her struggle with her parents, her identity and later supporting her mother with cancer and dealing with the loss of a loved one. It's an overall sad and touching story. The book is engaging and well written. The audiobook is narrated by Zauner herself.
