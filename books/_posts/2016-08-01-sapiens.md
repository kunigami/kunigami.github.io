<b>Sapiens</b> by Yuval Noah Harari. I rarely re-read books but I recall liking this book so much a few years back that I decided to revisit. I didn't remember a lot of the contents and wasn't as amused, possibly due to high expectations and maybe having internalized some of the more surprising facts. I do liked the idea of dedicating some time to re-read books I really liked, so I'll try to make a point of re-reading a book every year.
