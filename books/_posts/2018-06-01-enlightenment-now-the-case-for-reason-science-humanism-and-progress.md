<b>Enlightenment Now: The Case for Reason, Science, Humanism, and Progress</b> by Steven Pinker. It's a thorough presentation of facts and data to back the claim that despite localized set backs and short-term regressions, the world has been becoming more progressive. The idea that stuck the most with me is that each new generation tends to be more progressive than their parents, which shines a optimist light to a more humane future.
