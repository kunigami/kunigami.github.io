<b>The Overstory</b> by Richard Powers. Beautiful book and message. I like how a lot of the story happens around the Bay Area. I learned that in Stanford's <a href="https://trees.stanford.edu/treewalks/treemaps.htm" target="_blank">main quad</a> there are a variety of trees from all over the world. I thought the author went a bit overboard with esoteric words, and I had to look up the dictionary pretty often.
