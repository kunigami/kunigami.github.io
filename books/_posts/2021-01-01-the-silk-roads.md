<b>The Silk Roads</b> by Peter Frankopan. This book tells the history of world from the point of view of the region covered by the Silk roads, which include countries from the near and middle east and central asia. I don't recall learning so much history from a single book, and if I had to pick one, this would be my favorite book from 2020.
