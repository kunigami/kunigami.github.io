*Beyond the Sea* is a book by P. Singaram who wrote in Tamil. It was translated to English by Dr. R. Karthigesu. I bought this book in George Town in Malaysia at Areca Bookstore. This was one of my favorite purchases because Areca is also a publisher, so the titles there are hard to find elsewhere, so it felt like a unique purchase.

It is a historical fiction that happens during World War II after Japan had occupied Malaysia, in particular Penang. The second part happens the days after Japan surrenders.

## Plot

<spoiler>

The main character, Chellaia, is from Tamil Nadu. Before the war he had moved to Pengang to work at a money lender shop, owned by Vanayina, a friend of Chellaia's father. Vanayina took him under his wings as if an adopted son. Chellaia was also loved by Vanayina's wife, Kamatchi Ammal.
<br /><br />
During the war he joined INA, the Indian National Army, a nationalist force that fought alongside Japan to end British rule in India. After Japan surrendered, the members disbanded and he returned to Penang and tried to resume his life.
<br /><br />
Before he left, Vinayina intended to marry his daughter Maragathan to him. However, he strongly disapproved of him having joined the military and decided to marry her to another guy.
<br /><br />
The rest of the story is about Chellaia trying to persuade his boss to change his mind without success and then coming to terms with the reality. So it was not a happy ending.

</spoiler>

## Conclusion

Overall the plot is quite simple but I liked the historical background (I had never heard of INA), the depiction of daily life in post-war Penang, and the struggle between generations: the traditional Vanayina and the liberal Chellaia, and an impossible love.

I also chose this book because I wanted to get a better sense of Malaysia's Tamil population during a [recent trip](https://www.kuniga.me/trips/2026/10/12/malaysia.html). The story is set far enough in the past that it didn't really provide a lot of insights on the Tamil life in Malaysia today though.
