<b>Bury my Heart at Wounded Knee</b> by Dee Brown. The history of many native American tribes (Arapaho, Apache, Cheyenne, Kiowa, Navaho, Sioux) in the late 19th century and their fight against American settlers and military. It's a bit hard to read at times due to violent and injust acts of the latter. I wasn't familiar with this dark side of American history.
