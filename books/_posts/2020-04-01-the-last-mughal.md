<b>The Last Mughal</b> by William Dalrymple. Recounts the history of India, centered around the last years of Zafar's reign, and preceding the British Raj. I couldn't help drawing parallels with <i>Bury my Heart at Wounded Knee</i>. I picked this as part of the trip to India in 2019 - most of the book is in Delhi, so it is a good read if you're visiting the region.
